# mylife-alarms

Local Expo module that lets MyLife AI own its alarms.

- Android: `AlarmManager.setAlarmClock`, native persistence (SharedPreferences), boot/time-change rescheduling,
  a `specialUse` foreground service that rings (looping sound on the alarm stream, vibration, 30% to 100% volume ramp over 20 s,
  10 min auto-stop), a full-screen lock-screen Activity (Stop, Snooze 9 min), alarm notification channel.
- iOS 26+: AlarmKit, alert-only (Stop plus an "Open" secondary button). No snooze, no widget extension.
  Below iOS 26 the module loads and reports unsupported (`isAvailable()` is false, calls throw `ERR_UNSUPPORTED`).

Android ringing while the app is open: when an alarm fires with the app's activity in the foreground and the screen on, the
full-screen ringing screen is not launched. The service still rings and posts the notification (Stop / Snooze), and
`onAlarmFired` carries `inApp: true` so the app shows its own overlay. `setRingVolume(0..1)` ducks the alarm (respecting the
ramp, surviving pause/resume; lapses after 30 s unless re-asserted) so the app can listen for "AI" while it rings.

JS API: `src/index.ts` (`isAvailable`, `getPermissions`, `createAlarm`, `updateAlarm`, `deleteAlarm`, `listAlarms`,
`stopRinging`, `snooze`, `addListener`, permission helpers). Coded errors: `ERR_PERMISSION_REQUIRED`, `ERR_NOT_FOUND`,
`ERR_UNSUPPORTED`, `ERR_INVALID_ARGS`. Weekdays are ISO (1 = Monday ... 7 = Sunday), empty means one-time.

Config plugin (`app.plugin.js`, registered in `app.json`): Android permissions, copies `assets/sounds/android/alarm_<id>.wav`
to `res/raw`, adds `assets/sounds/ios/<id>.caf` to the main bundle, sets `NSAlarmKitUsageDescription`.
Receivers, service and activity are declared in `android/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml` (merged at build time).
Sounds are our own, generated by `scripts/alarm-sounds/generate.py`.
